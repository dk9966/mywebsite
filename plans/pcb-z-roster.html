PCB parts with Z height — roster

Plain English: anything soldered or mounted on a board has some thickness. This list orders typical maximum height above the PCB plane (package body + standing pins + tallest variants people actually use). Real boards vary by vendor; treat ranges as layout guides, not CAD tolerances.

Circuit scream is subjective: 10 = instant “that’s a board / computer,” 1 = could be almost anything.

Typical max Z (mm) Part / feature Scream (1–10) Notes Reference
0.2–0.6 0201 / 0402 / 0603 passives (R, C, L) 7 Density read — “rice grain city.” Almost no silhouette alone; power is massed repetition.
0.6–1.2 SMD diodes / small SOT packages 5 Low slabs; silhouette needs clusters or polarity silk to read beside denser passives.
0.9–1.4 QFN / MLF (pads under body; optional exposed pad) 10 Square “dark tile” from above; edge pads sometimes visible as a halo.
1.0–1.4 TSSOP / SSOP (two fine-pitch rows) 9 Strong train-track IC read from an angle; great paired rails for line tracing.
1.5–1.9 SOIC (wider two-row SO packages) 10 The everyday logic IC silhouette — readable without microscope.
1.8–2.6 QFP / LQFP / TQFP (gull leads on four sides) 11 The chip-city poster child — perimeter legs read instantly.
2–4 SMD tantalum (molded chip tantalum) 7 Directionality marked; reads as serious decoupling next to big ICs.
2–8 Stamped EMI shield / RF can 9 Boxy volumes with seams — great Z jumps at corners without fragile pins.
~2.4–4.5 M.2 2280 module 9 Low long slab — storage highway silhouette; pairs with PCIe lanes by association.
3.5–5 DIP IC (through-hole dual-inline) 10 Retro breadboard soul — parallel pin forest on two sides.
4–20 TO-220 / TO-247 (vertical regulator / FET + tab) 8 Tab as vertical plane — power-device silhouette everyone recognizes.
8–17 1.27 mm / 2.54 mm pin headers (vertical stacks) 8 Prototype DNA — rows of square pins; tallest when stacked or long tails.
8–15 CPU socket + retention frame / ILM 11 Mechanical ring around the die land — central plaza before cooler mounts.
11–14 RJ45 jack + magnetics block 10 Chunky cubic connector — network hardware identity.
~11 (+card) PCIe / PCI / edge connectors (slot + retention) 10 Gold fingers + slot mouth — interface machinery (photo: add-in card context).
13–17 Through-hole USB-A socket (stacked USB common) 9 Tall cage — swap in your own PCB-mount macro for a less catalog look.
20–55 Chipset / VRM / northbridge style heatsinks 11 Stepped extrusions — hero mesas without inventing fantasy buildings.
~30 DDR DIMM module (vertical UDIMM in socket) 10 Paired RAM slabs — tall-flank skyscraper language maps here.
40–165 CPU cooler (tower / downdraft / AIO block on bracket) 10 Usually wins tallest object on the board; dominates silhouette shots.

How to use this for your Tron mesh

  • Pick one spine (PCIe trace corridor or long DIMM channel) and hang four height tiers off it: passives + low packages → QFP/DIMM slabs → socket/heatsink mesas → cooler peak.
  • Repeat dimensions within a tier — that is how real boards stay legible.
  • If Z feels flat, add one EMI can, a dense passive or QFP cluster, and one vertical connector wall (RJ45/USB stack) before touching fantasy geometry.
