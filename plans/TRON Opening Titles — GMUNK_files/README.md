## The Output (01)

Images 03-06: the final rendered frames from the opening titles sequence.

## Opening Titles Overview (02)

Images 07-10: the light-wall evolution stills.

Joseph Kosinski communicated the motivation as evoking the sensations of the original light bike battle while slowly evolving the iconic light-walls into more complex patterns in all dimensions, ultimately revealing a city wireframe that transitioned seamlessly to the Vancouver BC skyline.

## Design Process (03)

Images 11-14: design iterations and line work.

munkowitz designed the line work in Illustrator, then added Z travel reliefs in Maya, and handed them over to David Lewandowski who animated each path by hand in Cinema4D using the mograph module, rendered with vray. The two exchanged scenes to ensure a strict grid framework and animation that synced to Flynn's voiceover.

Many detailing techniques were explored, including a 3D grid structure by Jake Sargeant and data point clouds, but Kosinski kept it clean and simple so it wouldn't date easily.

## Architecture 101 (04)

Images 15-19: the wireframe city buildings.

Kosinski (Architecture degree, Columbia) had the GFX team design all the graphic buildings from scratch, referencing the Vancouver skyline but mostly innovating. Very few revision cycles.

## Food for Thought (05)

Images 20-23: alternate angles and compositional explorations.

"Certainly would've been visually intriguing to put the camera at street level looking down the passageway of an emerging city, as the line work and graphic elements would have taken an immensely different aesthetic, mostly for beneficial results."
