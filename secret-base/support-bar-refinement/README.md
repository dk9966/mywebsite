# Workshop support and light refinement

The changes are installed and saved in the active scene,
`../platform-controls/cave-platform-sliders.blend`.
`undershelf-lights.blend` is a separate copy of the latest result.
`refined-supports.blend` preserves the first refinement.

- Seven continuous supports have longer, matching upper and lower angled ends.
- Their center channels are 0.090 units deep and 0.1056 units wide at the shaft.
- Shorter gray ledges end inside the supports and project less than their faces.
- Six wall lights are mounted beneath the ledges.
- The upper panels and overhead headers have a darker charcoal gray finish.

New parts live in `164 | Symmetric supports and light ledges` and follow the
existing platform parent. Replaced support and lamp meshes remain hidden.
`before-support-refinement.blend` preserves the complete original live state.

`undershelf-verification.json` records the latest mirror and closed mesh checks,
ledge/light clearances, and preservation of unrelated geometry and transforms.
`undershelf-lights-detail.png` shows the latest close render. The rendering script
changes only its background process. `before-ledge-and-channel-adjustment.blend`
preserves the live state before this follow-up adjustment.
