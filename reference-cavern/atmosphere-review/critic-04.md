# Fresh atmosphere critic 04

- Rock: **5.5/10** — repeated rectangular ledges and evenly stacked protrusions look constructed.
- Lighting: **7/10** — broad darkness succeeds; lit areas need stronger direction/falloff.
- Metal: **7/10** — restrained reflections and mottling; wear too broadly distributed.
- Accents: **7.5/10** — central focus; mast and workbench compete.
- Water: **8.5/10** — dark subordinate water with small ripples and restrained highlights.
- Depth: **6.5/10** — silhouettes and blue background work; middle-distance masses merge into black.

Top three actions:
1. Replace stacked-block rock rhythm with unequal strata, rounded fractures, recessed seams and occasional broad faces, prioritizing the left column, pendants and right wall.
2. Add restrained blue-gray haze behind successive middle silhouettes, reducing contrast into the distance while retaining foreground blacks and subdued water.
3. Tighten floor falloff, soften mast/workbench highlights, and concentrate wear at edges, seams and traveled areas.
