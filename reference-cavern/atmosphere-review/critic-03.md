# Fresh atmosphere critic 03

- Rock: **6/10** — broad vertical facets and stretched pointed formations lack broken shelves and erosion depth.
- Lighting: **7/10** — dark regions separate islands, but decks remain too even and atmospheric light limited.
- Metal: **6/10** — similar smooth finishes; wear and roughness variation hard to distinguish.
- Focal hierarchy: **6.5/10** — bright right ring and vivid peripheral accents compete with central monitors.
- Water: **6/10** — broad pale central reflection remains cloudy and distracting.
- Depth: **6.5/10** — black foreground and blue distance separate, but intermediate atmosphere remains weak.

Highest-impact actions:
1. Break vertical rock facets with stepped ledges, recessed fractures, rounded erosion and local overhangs. Vary stratum thickness/direction.
2. Concentrate light at the focal island, reduce peripheral fill/accents, and introduce tighter pools and faint localized haze. Reduce distant contrast progressively.
3. Replace the pale water band with sparse sharp directional reflections and broad dark intervals.
