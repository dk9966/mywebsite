# Fresh atmosphere critic 05

- Rock: **5/10** — smooth, vertically stretched, sparsely fractured exposed faces.
- Lighting: **7/10** — substantial darkness, but broad even illumination remains on floors and background.
- Metal: **6/10** — cloudy floor mottling competes with fine wear.
- Accents: **7/10** — central focus, but repeated cyan/orange elements distribute attention.
- Water: **8/10** — subordinate and legible; somewhat uniform ripples.
- Depth: **7/10** — good foreground/background separation; haze and simple contours flatten middle distance.

Highest-impact actions:
1. Increase rock complexity at multiple scales with layered ledges, fractures, undercuts and localized erosion, breaking long smooth lit contours.
2. Concentrate light and accents through tighter pools, steeper falloff, less even background illumination and selected dim peripheral accents.
3. Reduce cloudy floor mottling in favor of restrained roughness changes, fine use-directed scratches, occasional edge wear and larger calm surfaces.
