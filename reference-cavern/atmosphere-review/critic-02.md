# Fresh atmosphere critic 02

Scope excludes content, layout, architecture, labels and screen color choice.

- Natural layered/eroded rock: **5/10** — repetitive rounded ribs and smooth tapers.
- Isolated light pools and falloff: **7/10** — good darkness, but some pools too even.
- Subdued worn metal: **6/10** — restrained reflections; wear barely visible.
- Selective accent hierarchy: **7/10** — central focus, but circular display competes.
- Dark crisp secondary water: **4/10** — broad bright blurry column.
- Vast mysterious depth: **6/10** — foreground overlap, insufficient intermediate layers.

Highest-impact discrepancies:
1. Break continuous rock ribs into offset strata, fractured shelves and recesses, combining broad erosion and smaller angular damage.
2. Reduce pale water illumination, sharpen broken reflections and ripples, and localize mist.
3. Reveal intermediate silhouettes with progressively softer contrast and restrained distant light, retaining black foreground edges.
