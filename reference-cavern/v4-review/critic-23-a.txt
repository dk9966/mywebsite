**Overall: 7.3/10.** The candidate captures the layout and underground sci-fi setting well, but falls short of 8.5 mainly in rock construction, lighting, and camera perspective. Smooth matte surfaces and modular pieces are appropriate; texture roughness is not the issue.

1. **Rock pieces still read as separate stacked boxes.** Repeated tall rectangular blocks, conspicuous gaps, and isolated protrusions make the walls and central pillar resemble an assembly of blocks rather than dense rock. Pack modules more tightly, reduce deep seams, and mix shorter, wider blocks with subtle chipped edges. Keep the matte finish.

2. **Lighting loses the target’s atmospheric depth.** The candidate has large areas of nearly black rock, while its central light shaft has a conspicuous straight-sided silhouette. Broaden and soften the overhead illumination, add restrained cool fill to reveal rock planes, and let haze separate foreground, platforms, and distant walls. Preserve the workshop’s warm light.

3. **Camera and roof proportions need closer matching.** The image aspect is close, but the candidate feels more steeply downward-looking: platform tops are more exposed, and the lower-right approach is strongly foreshortened. Reduce the downward pitch and tune framing without moving platforms. Break the roof’s heavy, comparatively continuous band into tightly packed, stepped overhangs with the target’s varied recesses.
